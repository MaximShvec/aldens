(05)About Aldens

Since 2001,
a Riga manufacturer
for Latvia and Europe.

Aldens Holding specialises in aluminium constructions — windows and doors, façades, sliding and fire-resistant systems, office partitions — and also makes PVC and wooden constructions, all-glass structures, garage doors and canopies. We glaze factories, shopping centres, schools, kindergartens, hospitals and private homes.

We keep a close eye on materials, buy from leading European suppliers, and stand behind the result with a 5-year warranty on the complete window — frame, fittings and glass.

  1. 01

    Project discussion

    We review the drawings and your requirements — whether series-built, private or non-standard.

  2. 02

    System & measurements

    The right profile system is chosen and the openings are measured.

  3. 03

    Production in Riga

    Constructions are made at our factory under ISO 9001 quality control.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    On time — we respect the order deadlines.

  5. 05

    Installation

    Carried out by our team when you need it.
