(05)About Aldens

Since 2001,
a reliable manufacturer
for Northern Europe.

Aldens Holding manufactures aluminium structures, steel sheet doors and wooden doors for the European and Scandinavian markets. All three production facilities are in Riga, Latvia, and every year we improve our lines and equipment.

We monitor materials and quality at every production stage, buy from leading European suppliers, and stand behind the result with a 5-year warranty on the complete window — including fittings and glass.

  1. 01

    Brief & drawings

    Send drawings or a specification. We reply with a system recommendation and a quote.

  2. 02

    Engineering

    Fire, thermal and structural requirements matched to a profile system.

  3. 03

    Production in Riga

    Fabrication, glazing and finishing at our own facilities, with quality control at each stage.

  4. 04

    Delivery & installation

    Full or partial installation by our crews, anywhere in Europe and Scandinavia.

  5. 05

    Warranty

    5 years on the complete window; 5 + 2 years on steel doors.
